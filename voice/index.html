    Exercise library

    What a voice teacher rotates through beyond the daily core. Your coach picks a few each day based on your weak spots; locked exercises open as you level up.

    Posture & release

    Do this before you sing. Tension in your jaw, tongue, neck, and shoulders shows up as strain and off-pitch notes.

    1. Feet hip-width apart, weight balanced, knees soft
    2. Roll your shoulders up, back, and let them drop
    3. Let your jaw hang loose and gently massage the hinge
    4. Slow neck stretches: ear toward each shoulder
    5. Big yawn, then sigh from high to low
    6. Stand tall, as if a string lifts the top of your head

    Clean onsets

    Short, bouncy "ha" notes on do-mi-sol-mi-do, like a light laugh. Start each one right on pitch with a tiny puff of air, without sliding up into it.

    Top-note check

    Sing your current top note lightly on "oo" and hold it. It counts when it's within ±20¢, steady, and easy. Three easy days unlock the next half step up. Go up through a lighter, heady sound, never by pushing chest voice.

    Interval quiz

    No singing. Hear two notes and name the jump. Ten questions; the choices widen as you level up.

    Vowel tuning

    Hold one note and move through ee, eh, ah, oh, oo, about 2 seconds each. The pitch should stay put; the app shows which vowels pull you flat or sharp.

    Hold & swell

    The classic messa di voce. Hold one note (E3) for 8 seconds: start soft, swell louder, then fade back to soft, keeping the pitch perfectly still. Follow the curve.

    Bridge the break

    A slow do-mi-sol-do′ arpeggio on "nay", placed so the top crosses your break. Keep it light and let your voice tip over at the top instead of pushing your chest voice up.

    Your roadmap

    Typical timelines at about 15–20 focused minutes a day, 5 days a week, from voice teachers and practice data. Goals need both the practice days and a passing level check, so they only tick once your singing actually gets there.

      Ground rules

      • Sing at talking volume. Loud doesn't help while you're learning.
      • If it hurts or your voice goes hoarse, stop and rest a day. Hoarse for more than two weeks? Get it checked by a doctor.
      • Sip water, and hum or swallow instead of clearing your throat.
      • Ten minutes every day beats an hour once a week.
      • No sound on iPhone? Turn off silent mode.
