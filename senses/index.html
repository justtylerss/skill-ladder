🎒 Your senses

How Sense Quest works
  • Only methods with evidence. Each exercise has a chip: strong (meta-analysis or large trial), good, with a caveat, or promising (small or early studies). Tap it to see why it works, the numbers, and the papers.
  • Your first score is your baseline. Most measures depend on your screen, headphones, and room, so the app compares you with yourself. Use the same device, brightness, and distance each time.
  • Short and often. The studies used a few weeks of regular sessions; gains fade if you stop.
  • Safety first: sounds at a quiet-conversation volume, balance work next to a counter, essential oils only in jars, food-grade tastes you sip and spit. Sudden changes in any sense are a reason to see a professional, not to train.

0 of 5 quests complete

  1. 👁️sight

    Sight

    4–5 min

    Speed Sight

    A shape in the center and a star near the edge flash together. Name the shape, then tap where the star was. The flash shortens as you get both right.

    Contrast Hunt

    A faint striped patch appears. Is it tilted left or right? It fades as you get it right. Use the arrow keys on a computer.

  2. 👂hear

    Hearing

    3–4 min

    Pitch Duel

    Two tones. Which one is higher? The difference shrinks as you get it right. Keys 1 and 2 work too.

    Sound Compass

    Headphones on. A burst of sound comes from somewhere around your head; tap the direction.

    🙂

  3. 👃smell

    Smell

    2–10 min

    Scent Memory

    Put each scent in two small cups or tins (4 pairs), cover them with foil, and mix them up. Find the pairs by sniffing, two at a time, like the memory card game. Then try naming each one.

    Quick jars

    Four jars: rose, lemon, eucalyptus, clove (a drop of oil on a cotton pad, or the real thing). Ten seconds each, morning and evening.

  4. 🦶balance

    Balance

    2–5 min

    Steady Stance

    Four stances, two sets of 30 seconds each, next to a counter. They step up as your eyes-closed test improves.

    Quick Step

    Stand in the middle of a clear space. When the app calls a direction, step that way fast, then step back. Two minutes.

    Eyes-closed test

    Once a week: one leg, eyes closed, arms crossed, barefoot. Best of three, up to 45 seconds.

  5. ✋touch

    Touch & taste

    1–10 min

    Touch Lab

    With a partner and a paper clip bent so its two tips are a set gap apart. Close your eyes; your partner presses the tips on your fingertip, along it or across it, and you say which. Your partner taps the answer.

    Taste Ladder

    Make a stock, then halve it five times (half stock, half water) for six steps. Start at the weakest. Sip, swish, spit, rinse with water between cups.

    Quick Tap

    Tap the box the instant it turns green. Ten taps; your median counts.

Perfect Pitch Project

Name the note you hear. You start with three notes and gain one each time you score 85% or more.

Eye care habits

  • 20-20-20: every 20 minutes of screen time, look about 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
  • Daylight: outdoor time matters most for children's eyes; for adults it's still good for sleep and mood.
  • Regular eye exams: training can't replace glasses or catch eye disease.

    🛡️ Sense rules

    • Essential oils stay in jars: never on skin, never swallowed.
